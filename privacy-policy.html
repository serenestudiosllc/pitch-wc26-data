Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Cup Guide 26 ("the App") is developed by Serene Studios. This policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration required.

Data Fetched From the Internet

The App retrieves publicly available data to provide its features:

  • Match schedules and scores — sourced from the ESPN public API and a Serene Studios GitHub data repository.
  • News articles — sourced from public RSS feeds (e.g., FIFA, The Guardian, L'Équipe, AS, Lance!). Article links open in an in-app browser; Serene Studios does not track which articles you read.
  • Player and team information — bundled with the App or fetched from Wikipedia's public API for player photos.

All network requests are read-only. The App does not send any user data to external servers.

Motion Sensors

The App may access your device's accelerometer and gyroscope to detect shake gestures for interactive features. This data is processed entirely on-device and is never stored or transmitted.

Analytics

The App may use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (e.g., which screens are viewed). This data is not linked to any individual user and is used solely to improve the App. No advertising identifiers are used.

Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may send match reminders. Your device token is used only to deliver notifications and is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Widgets

The App offers a Home Screen widget that displays upcoming match information. Widget data is sourced from the same public APIs described above and is processed on-device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
